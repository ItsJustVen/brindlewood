Aldorite and Infernium are BrindleWood's own metals. They sit above netherite, and they're used to craft the strongest tools and weapons on the server.

## Two ways to get an ingot

**1. From crates.** Ingots drop ready-made from these [crates](/guides/crates-and-keys):

| Crate | Aldorite Ingot | Infernium Ingot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rare | 1 | – |
| Epic | 1 | 1 |
| Superior | 2 | 1 |

They're one prize among many in each crate, so a drop is never guaranteed.

**2. Crafted from dungeon drops.** The bosses in the [dungeons](/guides/dungeons) drop the parts. You craft the ingot yourself on a crafting table.

| Ingot | Craft it with |
| --- | --- |
| Aldorite Ingot | 4 Moon Dust, 4 Netherite Ingots and 1 Nether Star |
| Infernium Ingot | 4 Fire Dust, 4 Netherite Ingots and 1 Aldorite Ingot |

So Infernium is built on top of Aldorite. You need an Aldorite Ingot first.

> **Recipes are locked.** You have to unlock each recipe before you can craft it. Ask in **#help-us** on Discord if you're not sure how to unlock yours.

## What the bosses drop

Boss drops go to the player who kills the boss.

| Dungeon | Boss | Drop | Chance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sanctum of Skulls | Skeleton and Piglin bosses | Moon Dust | 40% |
| Sanctum of Skulls | Zombie and Pillager bosses | Aldorite Crystal | 5% |
| Whispering Maze | Skeleton and Vindicator bosses | Fire Dust | 30% |
| Whispering Maze | Pillager and Evoker bosses | Infernium Crystal | 5% |

Moon Dust and Fire Dust are what you need for the ingots. **Crystals can't be crafted**, so a boss drop or a crate is the only way to get one.

## What they make

Both metals make the same set of gear, in an Aldorite and an Infernium version: swords, long swords, daggers, spears, cleavers, battle axes, axes, lumber axes, hatchets, pickaxes, shovels, hoes, sickles, chisels, mining hammers, ore hammers and excavators.

Most pieces are made from ingots and a crystal. For example, an Aldorite Sword takes a Netherite Sword, 2 Aldorite Ingots and an Aldorite Crystal. Infernium gear works the same way with Infernium parts.

## Tips

- Save your Netherite Ingots. Each ingot uses 4, and crates also give them.
- Get Aldorite first. It's the only way into Infernium.
- Dungeons are hard. Bring friends, food and spare armor. See [Dungeons](/guides/dungeons).
- Found a bug or a missing recipe? Post it in **#help-us** on Discord.
