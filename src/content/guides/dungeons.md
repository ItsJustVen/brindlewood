BrindleWood has two dungeons in Aldoria. Each one is a place with its own enemies, a boss and its own loot.

| Dungeon | Difficulty |
| --- | --- |
| Sanctum of Skulls | I (Easy) |
| Whispering Maze | II (Medium) |

Use `/warp` to find them. The dungeon NPCs are in the spawn world at **-471, 72, -165** and **-630, 69, 108**. A guard NPC stands outside each one and can tell you everything you need to know.

## How a run works

Dungeons run in rounds:

1. The dungeon is awakening, waiting for adventurers.
2. A conquest begins. You fight your way through stages of mobs.
3. Each stage you finish drops rewards in the main area. The deeper you get, the bigger the reward.
4. When the conquest ends, the dungeon goes on cooldown. A new awakening starts when the cooldown is over.

The guard's info menu shows the current state: awakening, in a conquest, or on cooldown. It also lists the mobs and what each one drops.

## Before you go in

- Difficulty is Hard. Bring food, potions and spare armor.
- Team up. More players means the mobs go down faster.
- Don't leave right before the midnight EST restart.
- Dungeon drops sell well at the [Auction House](/guides/auction-house).
- Found a bug? Post it in **#bug-reports** on our Discord.

## Sanctum of Skulls (Difficulty I, Easy)

A good first dungeon. Learn how dungeons work here before you try the Whispering Maze.

**Guard:** Travis Woods stands alone outside the sanctum. **Mobs:** Zombies, Skeletons, Pillagers and Piglins, plus a boss.

Each stage you complete drops:

- Coal: 20 to 30
- Gold ingots: 5 to 8
- Emeralds: 2 to 4
- An enchanted book

And a chance at a bonus: a Common Key or a Rare Key. Rewards are dropped in the main area of the dungeon, so grab them before you leave.

**Good for:** early gold, emeralds and enchanted books, and a free shot at crate keys.

## Whispering Maze (Difficulty II, Medium)

A bigger fight than the Sanctum. Bring friends.

**Guard:** James Norton watches from above the maze. He has never gone inside and he never will. **Mobs:** Skeletons, Husks, Pillagers, Vindicators and Evokers, each with their own boss.

Each stage you complete drops:

- Iron ingots: 12 to 20
- Gold ingots: 8 to 12
- Diamonds: 1 to 3
- An enchanted book

And a chance at a bonus: a Common Key or a Superior Key. Each mob has its own drops, which you can browse in the guard's menu.

**Good for:** diamonds, iron and a shot at a Superior Key.
