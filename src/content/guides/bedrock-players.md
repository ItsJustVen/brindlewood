Bedrock players on console, mobile and Windows all join the same way, through a **friend account** instead of an address.

## Joining

1. Open Minecraft, press **Play**, then **Social** (top right).
2. Search for and add the friend account **EuphoriaHQ7845**.
3. Join through their game and accept the resource pack.

Bedrock works on the newest Minecraft version only. The full steps are on the [Getting Started](/play) page.

## The BR_ prefix

Bedrock names start with **BR_** on the server. So a Bedrock player called Steve is **BR_Steve**.

Use the full name, with the prefix, when you type a Bedrock player's name in a command:

- `/tpa BR_Steve`
- `/trade BR_Steve`
- `/pay BR_Steve 100`
- `/msg BR_Steve hello`
- Guild invites and bounties

## Good to know

- Bedrock has its own resource pack. It downloads automatically. See [The Resource Pack](/guides/resource-pack).
- Not sure of someone's name? `/list` shows who's online, and `/realname <name>` shows a player's real name.
- Use your **BR_** name on the vote sites and the store too. See [Voting](/guides/voting).
- Linking your Discord account works for Bedrock as well. See [Link Your Account](/guides/link-your-account).
- Trouble on Bedrock? Open a ticket in our Discord and tell us your device.
