Guilds have 15 levels. They earn XP just by being online: **every 20 minutes, each guild member who's online adds 1 XP** to the guild. The more of you online, the faster you grow.

## XP needed for each level

| Level | XP needed | Level | XP needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 20 | 9 | 1,150 |
| 3 | 50 | 10 | 1,500 |
| 4 | 100 | 11 | 2,250 |
| 5 | 175 | 12 | 3,750 |
| 6 | 300 | 13 | 5,500 |
| 7 | 500 | 14 | 7,500 |
| 8 | 750 | 15 | 10,000 |

## Rewards for levelling up

- **+1 member slot** at every level from 2 to 15
- New badge patterns at almost every level
- New badge border styles at levels 3, 6, 9, 12 and 15

A new guild starts with 3 member slots, so a max-level guild can hold a lot of people.

## Tips

- Recruit people who actually play. Online time is what levels a guild.
- Check the guild menu to see your XP and your next reward.
