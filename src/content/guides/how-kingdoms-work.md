## Which kingdom am I in?

Your kingdom is **wherever you settle**. There is no list to sign up for. Build or claim land in a kingdom's corner and you live there.

## What a kingdom gives you

- Each kingdom is run as a [guild](/guides/guilds), so it has a **shared bank** and a **shared ender chest**.
- The **leader** decides the kingdom's laws, so ask before you build or change things.
- There are no other built-in perks. A kingdom is about the community you choose.

## Getting there

There are no server warps for the kingdoms. Use a **player warp** (`/pw`) if a player has made one, or find the corner on the [Live Map](https://map.brindlewood.net).

## Moving to another kingdom

You can move any time, but you can only bring what fits in your **inventory and ender chest**. Builds and other structures stay where they are, and you have to **unclaim your land** in the kingdom you're leaving. See [Claiming Land](/guides/claiming-land).

## Leading a kingdom

Rosewood and Subterra have no leader yet. To take one, open a ticket in our Discord with a **name**, a **theme** and **how you plan to rule**. A new leader can rename the kingdom to fit their theme.
