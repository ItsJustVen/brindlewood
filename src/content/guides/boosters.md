Boosters last **1 hour** once activated.

- **Experience booster:** 2x vanilla XP
- **Level XP booster:** 2x Level XP
- **Discount booster:** 20% off NPC prices in spawn

Boosters can drop from crates. See [Crates and Keys](/guides/crates-and-keys). You can also buy them in the [CoinShop](/guides/coinshop).

## Tips

- Use an XP booster before a long dungeon or grind session.
- Use a discount booster before big NPC buys like backpack tiers or horses.
