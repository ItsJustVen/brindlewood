## Your player profile

Open it with `/menu`. It shows your rank, your character level and XP, your playstyle, and your balances: money, bank and coins.

From the profile you can reach your levels, achievements, settings and skill tree. Each also has its own command: `/levels`, `/achievements`, `/settings`. Custom crafting recipes you've unlocked are in `/recipes`.

The Leaderboards NPC is in the spawn world at **-64, 99, 3**. Or check `/leaderboards` to see how you rank against everyone else: weekly, monthly and all time.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/menu` | Opens your profile |
| `/levels` | Your character levels and rewards |
| `/traits` | Your traits |
| `/skills` / `/skilltree` | Your skills and the skill tree |
| `/achievements` | Your achievements |
| `/settings` | Your settings |
| `/recipes` | Unlocked custom recipes |
| `/playstyles` | Pick your playstyle |
| `/leaderboards` | Server leaderboards |

## Traits

Traits are passive perks, and there are over 60 of them. Open them with `/traits`.

Each trait has tiers: **Bronze, Silver, Gold and Master**. A higher tier is stronger. You collect trait tiers from:

- **Character levels:** 28 of the level rewards upgrade or unlock a trait
- **Your job and playstyle**, which can add to some traits

Traits come in categories such as Social, Athletic, Magical, Resource, Navigation and Attacking.

## Some examples

- **Trustworthy** (Social): bigger bank loans and higher savings interest, from +0.5% at Bronze up to +2.0% at Master
- **Negotiator** (Social): cheaper NPC prices, 10% off at Bronze and 20% off at Silver
- **Pack Mule** (Athletic): lets you buy bigger backpacks, with tier 3 at Bronze and tier 4 at Silver
- **Divine Recovery** (Magical): Regeneration II when your health gets very low
- **Immortal Stomach** (Magical): eat rotten flesh safely
- **Acrobat** (Athletic): less fall damage

Others include Mining Vision, Loot Slayer, Bait Master, Cartographer, Trader and Gambler. Open `/traits` to see what each tier does.
