- `/map` opens the world map menu (overworld, nether and end).
- `/claims` shows claims around you.

Use the map to find towns, and the claim map before you build so you don't overlap someone else.

## Claim map colours

Each square on the claim map is one chunk.

| Colour | Name | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brown | Wilderness | Nobody has claimed this chunk |
| Red | Visitor | Another player owns this chunk |
| Yellow | Member | You've been added to this chunk |
| Green | Owner | You own this chunk |

The corner box pieces mark **your position** on the map.

There is also a **Live Map** of the whole server on the web: [map.brindlewood.net](https://map.brindlewood.net).

> Tip: look at the claim map before you claim. See [Claiming Land](/guides/claiming-land).
