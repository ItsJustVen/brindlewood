As you play, your character levels up. Every level has a reward waiting for you, and you can claim them from the **Levels** menu in your player profile.

> Important: claim rewards in order. You have to claim your previous level's reward before the next one.

## What levels give you

- Coins, money and crate keys
- Homes, auction slots, player warp slots and job slots
- Commands like `/disposal`, `/nick` and `/back`
- Crafting recipes, tool skins and profile traits

Everything here is free to earn. No store purchase needed.

## Commands you unlock

These commands can be bought with coins in the [CoinShop](/guides/coinshop), but you can also unlock them free by levelling:

| Command | Unlocks at level |
| --- | --- |
| `/disposal` | 4 |
| `/nick` | 11 |
| `/back` | 17 |
| `/craft` | 24 |
| `/feed` | 31 |
| `/enderchest` | 39 |

**Job slots:** your second job slot unlocks at level 19, and your third at level 49.

## Coins from levelling

- 50 coins: levels 2 and 6
- 75 coins: levels 9 and 12
- 100 coins: levels 14 and 16
- 150 coins: levels 21 and 24
- 200 coins: levels 26 and 28
- 250 coins: levels 33 and 34
- 300 coins: levels 38 and 40
- 350 coins: levels 45 and 48
- 400 / 450 / 500 coins: levels 50, 54 and 58

That adds up to **4,300 free coins**.

## More level rewards

- **Homes:** +1 home slot at 26 different levels, starting at level 1
- **Auction slots:** +1 at levels 4, 13, 31, 37, 42, 48 and 55
- **Player warps:** +1 at levels 8, 25 and 46
- **Tool skins:** a new one every few levels, starting at level 4

## Crate keys

- Common: levels 2, 5, 7 and 12
- Rare: levels 10, 15, 17, 20 and 24
- Epic: levels 22, 27, 29, 32, 34, 36, 39 and 44
- Superior: levels 41, 49, 51, 53, 57 and 59
