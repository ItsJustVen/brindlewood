BrindleWood has about **80 custom enchantments** on top of vanilla, powered by ExcellentEnchants. Many have a **chance** to trigger, and the chance and strength usually go up with the enchant's level.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/excellentenchants list` | Lists every custom enchantment and what it does |

## Where to find them

- Enchanting tables
- Loot found in the world
- Gear carried by mobs

They show up on books and gear like any enchant. The tables below give the highest level each one can reach.

## Curses

Curses are bad. Check an item before you use it.

| Curse | Max | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Curse of Breaking | 3 | Chance to use extra durability |
| Curse of Fragility | 1 | The item can't be put through a grindstone or anvil |
| Curse of Misfortune | 3 | Chance of no drops from blocks or mobs |
| Curse of Mediocrity | 3 | Chance to disenchant item drops |
| Curse of Death | 3 | Killing a player can make you die too |
| Curse of Drowned | 3 | Chance to fish up a Drowned Zombie |

> Enchants that give a potion effect show the exact effect and how long it lasts in the item's lore, so hover over the item to check.
