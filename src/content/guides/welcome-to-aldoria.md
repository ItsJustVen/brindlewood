**Welcome to Aldoria.**

A land shaped by centuries of struggle, Aldoria bears the marks of ancient wars between mankind and monsters. Though the great towns now stand strong, their streets free of fear, the wild places beyond still whisper of darker days.

In the deep forests, the forgotten ruins, and the yawning mouths of hidden dungeons, danger stirs. Creatures born of old chaos still roam, cloaked in shadow and legend.

Beyond the safety of Aldenburg's walls, or the bustling shores of Windshore, the woods of Grinwood and the depths of Deepstone Cave call to those brave, or desperate, enough to listen.

You arrive in a world that has fought for its peace, yet trembles on the edge of unknown perils. Whether you seek fortune, sanctuary, or glory, know this: Aldoria is not tamed. It never will be.

**The forests remember. The rivers murmur of secrets. The earth hides its scars.**

Tread carefully, adventurer, for though the towns sleep in comfort, the wilds still hunger.
