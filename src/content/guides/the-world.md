BrindleWood has two main worlds.

**Aldoria** (the spawn world) is the hub. Use `/spawn` to get there. It's where the NPCs, the bank, the jobs and the dungeons are. PvP is off here. It's for exploring and services, not for building.

**Playworld** is where you build, claim land and make your home. Everything you place and claim lives here.

## Server settings

- **Difficulty:** Hard. Mobs hit harder and hunger matters, so bring real food and gear.
- **Nether:** enabled. You can't claim land there.
- **PvP:** on in playworld, off in Aldoria. Claim owners can turn it on or off inside their own claims.
- **Restarts:** daily at midnight EST. Finish dungeon runs and trades before the restart.
