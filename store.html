Support Without Buying the Win

Nearly everything in the store can also be earned by playing, including ranks, claims, fly, crates, and cosmetics. Buying is simply a shortcut that helps keep BrindleWood running.

The two exceptions are Spawner Core (extra spawner permissions, limited because too many spawners cause lag) and premium races.

Buying is never required. Inviting a friend, voting, sharing feedback, or just playing all help BrindleWood grow.
