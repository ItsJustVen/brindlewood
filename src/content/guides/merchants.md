Merchants buy and sell goods, and prices move with demand.

- Each merchant shows a **Demand rating from 1 to 5** in their menu.
- Lots of recent buying raises prices. Quiet days can bring them down.
- Stock refreshes about every 11 minutes, so check back.
- Merchants never lower prices for sentiment, and you can never be completely sure what they'll do.

## Where to find them

In the spawn world (Aldoria):

- Merchant 1: **108, 90, 76**
- Merchant 2: **-562, 72, 189**
- Merchant 3: **-120, 81, 431**

## Tips

- Check the Demand rating before you buy or sell.
- If a price is high, wait for the stock to refresh or try another merchant.
- Merchants need access. If a merchant won't open for you, you may need to unlock it first.
- The Negotiator trait gets you discounts.
