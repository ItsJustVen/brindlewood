Jobs are the main way to earn money ($) by playing normally. Mine, farm, fish or fight, and get paid for it.

**Start here:** each job has its own NPC in the spawn world (`/spawn`). Find the NPC to join. Money arrives as you do the job's actions.

## Joining and leaving

Jobs are **not** joined with commands. Walk up to the job's NPC in the spawn world (`/spawn`) and use its menu to join, leave or check your progress.

## How jobs work

- You start with **1 job slot**.
- Your 2nd slot unlocks at character level 19, and your 3rd at level 49.
- Every job levels up as you work it, up to **level 30**.
- Higher job level means higher pay: at level 30 you earn about 3.9x what you earned at level 1.
- There is no hourly earnings cap.

## Tips

- Pick the job that matches what you already enjoy.
- Some jobs pay more per action (like Enchanter and Fighter); others pay less per action but are easy to do all day (like Farmer and Lumberjack).
- Spend your money at the [Auction House](/guides/auction-house), [Player Shops](/guides/player-shops), the Landlord's [chunk auctions](/guides/chunk-auctions) and the [horse stables](/guides/horses-and-stables).
- Don't forget the free hourly, daily and weekly rewards in `/rewards`.
