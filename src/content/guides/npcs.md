BrindleWood's towns are full of NPCs. Click one to open its menu. Each has a name and a job:

- **Merchants** buy and sell goods. Prices shift with demand. See [Merchants](/guides/merchants).
- **Vendors** sell supplies.
- **Blacksmiths** craft and repair for you.
- **Tailors** make backpacks and backpack upgrades.
- **Artists** give quests in return for rewards.
- **Sailors and Navigators** teleport you to far places.
- **Couriers, Bankers and Landlords** handle delivery, money and housing.
- **Horse Stables** sell horses. See [Horses and Stables](/guides/horses-and-stables).

Want a map? Use the [NPC finder](/npcs).

## Good to know

- Some NPCs only open for you once you have the right permission or level.
- Crafting NPCs have a **Workload**. The busier they are, the longer your order takes.
- The Negotiator trait gives you better NPC prices. See [Your Player Profile and Traits](/guides/player-profile-and-traits).
