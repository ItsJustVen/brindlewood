The most useful commands in one place. You can also type `/help` in-game to open the server guide menu.

## Getting around

- `/spawn` goes to Aldoria
- `/rtp` opens the random teleport menu
- `/map` opens the world map menu
- `/home`, `/sethome`, `/delhome`, `/homes`
- `/warp` for server warps, `/pw` or `/playerwarps` for player warps
- `/tpa`, `/tpahere`, `/tpaccept`, `/tpdeny`
- `/tptoggle` blocks teleport requests
- `/back` returns you to where you died or last teleported from (unlock it at level 17)

## Menus

- `/profile` (also `/pp` or `/menu`): your player profile
- `/levels`, `/traits`, `/achievements`, `/settings`, `/recipes`
- `/rewards`, `/kits`, `/jobs`, `/leaderboards`
- `/playstyles`, `/races gui`, `/races classgui`
- `/guilds` (guild menu) and `/guild` (create or manage yours)
- `/coinshop`, `/cosmetics`, `/toolskins`, `/tags`, `/pets`
- `/auction` or `/ah` for the Auction House

## Money and social

- `/balance` shows your money
- `/pay <player> <amount>` sends money
- `/trade <player>` starts a trade
- `/bounty <player> <amount>` places a bounty
- `/msg <player>` sends a private message, `/msgtoggle` turns messages off
- `/ignore <player>` ignores someone
- `/list` shows who's online, `/realname <name>` shows a player's real name

## Land, links and help

- `/claim`, `/unclaim`, `/claims`
- `/referral <username>` if a friend invited you
- `/store` opens our store
- `/discord` gives you the Discord link
- `/rules` shows the server rules
- `/bug` reports a bug
