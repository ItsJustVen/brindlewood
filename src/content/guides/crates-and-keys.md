Every crate can be unlocked with a key, and every key can be earned free. Odds are shown in-game when you preview a crate.

## The four main crates

Common, Rare, Epic and Superior. Plus the **Vote Crate** (from voting) and seasonal crates.

## What's inside

- Tools and weapons: diamond to netherite
- Materials: coal, iron, gold, emerald, diamond, netherite and more
- Money and coins
- Experience
- Tool skins
- Higher keys: Common can drop Rare and Epic keys, and so on
- Boosters and special items like totems, tridents, shulker boxes, nether stars and elytra
- 30-day ranks: a small chance at Lord, Paladin, Duke or King

Better crates give better rewards, more money and more coins.

## Getting keys

- **Free:** `/rewards`, character levels, referrals, dungeons and voting
- **CoinShop:** Common 50, Rare 100, Epic 200, Superior 400 coins
- **Store:** [brindlewood.tebex.io](https://brindlewood.tebex.io)

Use your keys at the crates. They're right behind you when you do `/spawn`.
