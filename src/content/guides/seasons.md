BrindleWood plays in **seasons**. When a season ends, the server resets.

## What resets

**Everything resets. Nothing stays.** Nothing carries over to the next season, including things you bought in the store. See [Refunds and Store Terms](/guides/refunds-and-store-terms).

## When does a season end?

The end date has not been set yet. When it is, we will announce it in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/aCjzjkNV7F).

## What this means for you

- Play for the fun of it. Your builds, items, money and progress are part of this season's story.
- Buying from the store is optional and never required. Purchases don't transfer to the next season.
