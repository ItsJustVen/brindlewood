Bedrock players can join and play. How to get in is covered on the [How to Play](/play) page.

## The BR_ prefix

Bedrock names start with **BR_** on the server. So a Bedrock player called Steve is **BR_Steve**.

Use the full name, with the prefix, when you type a Bedrock player's name in a command:

- `/tpa BR_Steve`
- `/trade BR_Steve`
- `/pay BR_Steve 100`
- `/msg BR_Steve hello`
- Guild invites and bounties

## Good to know

- Bedrock has its own resource pack. It downloads automatically. See [The Resource Pack](/guides/resource-pack).
- Not sure of someone's name? `/list` shows who's online, and `/realname <name>` shows a player's real name.
- Trouble on Bedrock? Open a ticket in our Discord and tell us your device.
