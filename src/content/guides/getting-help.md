Prefer the website? Use the [Contact page](/contact) to send a support request, suggestion, bug report or ban appeal straight to staff.

Or use the right channel in our Discord:

- **#create-a-ticket** for account problems, purchases, rollbacks and anything private
- **#bug-reports** to report bugs. For in-game help, find the **Server Guide** NPC in the spawn world at **-7, 99, 18**
- **#suggestions** for ideas
- **#applications** to apply for staff or roles
- **#patch-notes** to see what changed

## Before you ask

- Search these guides. Your question may already be answered.
- Restart your game if the resource pack seems stuck.
- Tell us your platform (Java or Bedrock) when you report a problem.
- Include what you were doing, where, and any screenshots.

The COMMUNITY role: you get it automatically. No application needed.
