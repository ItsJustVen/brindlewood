- `/tpa <player>` asks to teleport to them
- `/tpahere <player>` asks them to come to you
- `/tpaccept` or `/tpdeny` to answer

Requests expire after 2 minutes.

## Good to know

- Teleports have a 3-second delay. Stand still until it finishes.
- You can't teleport, fly or use commands while tagged in combat.
- `/back` returns you to where you died or last teleported from. Unlock it at level 17, or buy it in the [CoinShop](/guides/coinshop).
