Here is a simple order for your first hour. Each step links to the full guide if you want more.

> **The short version:** join, pick a race, claim your rewards, get a job, then claim land before you build. Everything else can wait.

## 1. Join and accept the resource pack

Join with the address or friend account on the [Getting Started](/play) page. When the resource pack prompt appears, accept it and wait. The first download takes up to about 3 minutes on Bedrock and 5 on Java. See [Resource Pack](/guides/resource-pack) if it seems stuck.

## 2. Pick your race

The race menu opens by itself the first time you join, and you can't play until you choose. **Your race is permanent**, so read each one first.

![The Choose Your Destiny menu, where you pick your race](/screens/guide-race-menu.webp)

More: [Choosing Your Race](/guides/choosing-your-race).

## 3. Collect your free rewards

Type `/rewards` and claim your hourly reward. The daily and weekly rewards give keys as well as money and coins.

![The Rewards menu with Hourly, Daily and Weekly rewards, plus Referrals and Voting](/screens/guide-rewards-menu.webp)

More: [Free Rewards](/guides/free-rewards). Linking your Discord account with `/link` also gives you a free Superior Key ([how](/guides/link-your-account)).

## 4. Get a job

Jobs are the main way to earn money. Each one has its own NPC in Aldoria, and some are a long walk from spawn. The Jobs NPC at **-53, 99, 19** shows your slots and can send you to your employer once you have chosen a job.

![The Jobs menu showing your job slots](/screens/guide-jobs-menu.webp)

More: [Jobs](/guides/jobs) has every NPC location and what each job pays. The [NPC finder](/npcs) shows them on a map.

## 5. Get to know spawn

Moonfall Town is the safe area around spawn. The bank, the auction house, the coin shop and the crates are all here. Beyond the safe zone, Aldoria has danger zones where mobs spawn and players can attack you, so don't wander far without gear.

More: [The World](/guides/the-world) and [Danger Zones and Safe Zones](/guides/danger-and-safe-zones).

## 6. Head to the playworld and claim your land

The playworld is where you build. Walk into the **RTP pond** at spawn (around 0, 97, 42) and a menu opens. Left-click the Overworld option to teleport to a random spot in a random Kingdom (it has a 5 minute cooldown). Want to see a particular Kingdom? Ask in that Kingdom's forum on our Discord and someone will show you around. When you find a spot you like, stand in the chunk and type `/claim`. Claiming is free, and you start with 1 claim and 10 chunks. **Claim before you build.** Unclaimed land can't be protected.

![The chat message and top bar after a chunk is added to a claim](/screens/guide-claim-chunk-added.webp)

More: [Claiming Land](/guides/claiming-land).

## 7. Set a home

Type `/sethome <name>` before you explore, so `/home <name>` can always bring you back.

More: [Homes](/guides/homes).

## If you die

Don't panic. Every death leaves a grave with your items where you fell. Get back to it quickly: other players can break it, and after **7 days** it's deleted, items included.

More: [Dying, Graves and Item Loss](/guides/dying-graves-and-item-loss).

## Then what?

- Stand in the [AFK Pond](/guides/afk-pond) for free money while you do something else.
- Join a [guild](/guides/guilds) or look at [how the Kingdoms work](/guides/how-kingdoms-work).
- Try the [Sanctum of Skulls](/guides/dungeons), the easier of the two dungeons.
- Stuck? Ask in our Discord or open a ticket. See [Getting Help](/guides/getting-help).
