Quests are part of the Races system. You get a chat message when one starts, and you can read any quest's objectives and rewards with `/races questview <quest name>`.

## Quest commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/races questlist` | Lists the quests |
| `/races questview <quest>` | Shows a quest's story and objectives |
| `/races questprogress <quest>` | Shows how far along you are |

Quest names have spaces, so write the whole name, like `/races questview Chef Challenge`.

## Quests for everyone

- **Beginning:** break 10 grass blocks and 10 dirt blocks for a diamond
- **Hunting:** become a master hunter
- **Explorer:** discover new horizons
- **Architect:** build masterful structures
- **Farmer's Duty:** harvest and cultivate
- **Treasure Hunter:** seek out hidden treasures
- **Chef Challenge:** prepare a feast fit for a king
- **Miner Madness:** mine rare ores in the depths

## Race quests

Every race has three quests of its own, including its Awakening quest.

| Race | Quests |
| --- | --- |
| Ignari | Awakening, Cinderheart, Wildfire's Crown |
| Zephyrin | Awakening, Stormrider, Skybound |
| Marisel | Awakening, Abyssal Descent, Leviathan's Bargain |
| Terrune | Awakening, Bedrock Vow, Mountain's Heart |

The premium races (Kaldrun, Vyressa, Aethran and Umbrath) have three each too. For the Awakened forms themselves, see [Awakened Races](/guides/awakened-races).
