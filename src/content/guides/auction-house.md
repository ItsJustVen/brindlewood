Sell items to other players, even while you're offline. Buy what other adventurers are selling. No shop needed.

**Open it:** `/ah` (also `/auction` or `/auctionhouse`), or visit the Auction NPC in the spawn world at **46, 100, 0**.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/ah` | Opens the Auction House |
| `/ah sell <price>` | Sells the item in your hand at a fixed price |
| `/ah sell <price> <amount>` | Sells only part of a stack |
| `/ah auction` | Opens a menu to start a bidding auction |
| `/ah bid <price>` | Places a bid on an auction |
| `/ah cancel` | Cancels one of your listings |
| `/ah collect` | Collects items that expired or were cancelled |
| `/ah amount` | Shows how many listings you have |
| `/ah search <name>` | Searches for an item or a seller |
| `/ah blacklist add <player>` | Stops a player from buying your listings |
| `/ah blacklist remove <player>` | Lets them buy again |
| `/ah blacklist list` | Shows who you've blocked |

## Selling an item

1. Hold the item (or stack) in your hand.
2. Type `/ah sell <price>` (example: `/ah sell 500`).
3. To sell only part of a stack: `/ah sell <price> <amount>`.
4. Your item appears in the Auction House for everyone.

## Buying an item

1. Type `/ah`.
2. Click the item you want.
3. Confirm. The money leaves your balance and the item goes to you.

## Bidding auctions

- Use `/ah auction` to start one, and `/ah bid <price>` to bid.
- When you bid, the money is taken from you right away.
- You can't bid on your own auction.
- You can cancel a bid you placed.

## Good to know

- Listings last **48 hours**. If nobody buys it, it expires.
- Expired or unsold items go back to you: use `/ah collect`.
- Money from sales is paid to you when your item sells.
- A **15% fee** is taken from the sale price, so price with that in mind.
- Every member can have **5 listings** at once. Character levels and ranks add more.
- Prices are in dollars ($), the same money you earn from jobs and rewards.

## Tips

- Check `/ah` first to see what similar items go for.
- Rare drops from dungeons and crates usually sell best.
- Sort the list inside the menu to find the cheapest or newest items.
