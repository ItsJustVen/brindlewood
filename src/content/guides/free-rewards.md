Everyone gets free rewards just for playing. Open the menu with `/rewards` and click to claim.

![The Rewards menu with Hourly, Daily and Weekly rewards, plus Referrals and Voting](/screens/guide-rewards-menu.webp)

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/rewards` | Hourly, daily and weekly rewards |
| `/kit` or `/kits` | Free kits |
| `/referral <username>` | Credit the friend who invited you |
| `/vote` | Shows the vote links |
| `/link` | Link your Discord account for a free Superior Key ([how](/guides/link-your-account)) |
| `/coinshop` | Spend your coins |

The Rewards NPC is in the spawn world at **14, 99, 16**.

| Reward | You get |
| --- | --- |
| Hourly | $100 + 1 coin |
| Daily | $500 + 5 coins + 1 Common Key |
| Weekly | $2,000 + 25 coins + 1 Rare Key |

The menu shows how long until each one is ready again. You have to come back and click to claim.

## Every night and every week

- **Nightly key drop:** every night at **10:30 PM Eastern**, a single Common Key drops at spawn. The **first player to pick it up** gets it, so be near spawn and quick.
- **Most Active Player:** each week's top player wins **50 coins and a cosmetic crate key**, given the next time they join after the week ends.

## Free kits

Use `/kits` to claim free kits:

- **Starter Kit:** basic tools. Every hour.
- **Food Kit:** basic food. Every hour.
- **PvP Kit:** gear for a fight. Every hour.

Rank kits (Knight to King) are covered in [Ranks](/guides/ranks).

## Referrals

Did a friend send you here? Type `/referral <their username>`.

- You both get **1 Common Key**.
- You can only enter a referral once.
- You can't refer yourself.

## More ways to earn for free

- **Voting:** every vote gives a Vote Crate key and Level XP. See [Voting](/guides/voting).
- **AFK Pond:** see [The AFK Pond](/guides/afk-pond).
- **Character levels:** coins, keys, homes and more. See [Character Levels](/guides/character-levels).
- **Jobs:** see [Jobs](/guides/jobs).
