## Warps

- `/warp` shows the server warps: towns, dungeons and key places
- `/warp <name>` takes you there
- `/spawn` takes you to spawn
- `/warp rw` takes you to the **resource world**

## Player warps

Let everyone teleport to a place you made.

- `/pw` opens and manages player warps (also `/pwarp` or `/playerwarps`)
- You start with 1 player warp slot

## More player warp slots

- **Character levels:** +1 slot at levels 8, 25 and 46
- **Ranks:** Knight +1, Lord +2, Paladin +2, Duke +3, King +3

> Tip: put your player warp next to your shop or your build so people can find it.

## The resource world

The resource world is for gathering. It **resets every Sunday** with a new seed, so new items and blocks from the latest Minecraft release show up. Don't build or leave anything there you want to keep.

- `/warp rw` takes you there
