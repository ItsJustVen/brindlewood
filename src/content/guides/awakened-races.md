Reach level 100 in your race to unlock its Awakened form. Every Awakened form gets:

- A passive bonus in its home terrain
- A chance at bonus drops that fit its theme
- Friendly mobs that won't attack you
- A special ability. Hold the ability item and **Shift + right-click in the air**. It uses up the item and has a cooldown.
- Bonus damage against certain other races

## Awakened Ignari

- **Passive:** Haste in the Nether
- **Ability:** hold a Blaze Rod. Sets mobs within 6 blocks on fire and damages them
- **Bonus drops:** blaze rods, magma cream, glowstone dust, nether wart
- **Friendly:** Blazes, Magma Cubes, Striders
- Can't eat pufferfish

## Awakened Zephyrin

- **Passive:** Jump Boost above Y 80
- **Ability:** hold a Feather. Launches you through the air where you're looking
- **Bonus drops:** honeycomb, honey bottles, phantom membranes, feathers
- **Friendly:** Parrots, Bees, Phantoms

## Awakened Marisel

- **Passive:** Conduit Power while in water
- **Ability:** hold a Heart of the Sea. Heals everyone within 10 blocks
- **Bonus drops:** kelp, sea pickles, prismarine shards, cod
- **Friendly:** Dolphins, Guardians, Elder Guardians, Cod, Salmon

## Awakened Terrune

- **Passive:** Haste below Y 40
- **Ability:** hold a Stone. Slams the ground and damages mobs within 6 blocks
- **Bonus drops:** extra iron, copper, gold, coal and ancient debris from ore
- **Friendly:** Iron Golems, Armadillos

## Premium Awakened abilities

- **Kaldrun:** a netherite nova that grants brief damage immunity
- **Vyressa:** throw a venomous flask made from a fermented spider eye
- **Aethran:** use an amethyst shard for brief levitation
- **Umbrath:** consume an ender pearl to blink forward

## Matchups

The four elemental races hit each other harder in a loop: **Ignari → Terrune → Zephyrin → Marisel → Ignari**. Awakened forms also get a smaller bonus against one premium race each.
