Minions work for you around the clock, even while you're offline. Buy one, place it, and collect what it gathers.

- There is no command to place them. Buy one, then place the minion item in the world like a block.
- You do not need to feed your minions. Hunger is turned off.
- Every minion has 5 levels (Farmer has 4).
- Each upgrade costs money and makes the minion faster, stronger and able to store more.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/minion shop` | Opens the minion shop to buy minions |
| `/minion myminions` | Shows your minions |
| `/minion` | Opens the minion menu |

## Setup and tips

- Click a minion to open its menu. That's where you collect items and upgrade.
- Link a chest within 48 blocks so it keeps gathering after its own storage fills.
- Give it a custom name (up to 32 characters).
- When storage is full, the minion stops. Empty it or link a chest.
- **Range** is how far the minion works around itself. Higher level, bigger area.
- **Speed** is how often it acts. Lower is faster.
- **Smelts** means it smelts what it gathers. **Auto-sells** means it sells for you.
- **Level XP:** minions also drop experience. Chance is 10% at level 1, going up 10% a level to 50% at level 5.

## Which should I buy first?

- Early game: Miner or Farmer, cheap and useful.
- Hands-off money: add a Seller and let drops turn into cash.
- Mob drops: Slayer with a Collector nearby.
- Wood: Lumberjack. Fish: Fisher.
- Feeder is the most specialised type. Its menu lists exactly what it does.
