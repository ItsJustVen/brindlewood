Guilds are groups of players who play, build and level together. They run on BetterTeams.

## Getting started

- Use `/guild` (`/team` works too) to create or manage your guild.
- Guild names are 2 to 12 characters.
- Invite friends, and manage your members, settings and badge in the guild menu.

## What you get

- Guild chat
- Allies: up to 5 allied guilds
- Guild members can't hurt each other
- A guild bank at the Banker, which earns interest
- Guild achievements
- A custom badge (patterns and borders)

## Guild bank

Your guild has its own bank account with the Banker. All deposits are tax-free, and a small tax applies on withdrawals. The guild interest rate is fixed. See [Banks and the Banker](/guides/banks).

See [Guild Levels and Rewards](/guides/guild-levels) for how guilds grow.
