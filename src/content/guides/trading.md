Want to swap items or sell something to a friend right now? Use a direct trade.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/trade <player>` | Sends a trade request |
| `/pay <player> <amount>` | Sends money without a trade window |
| `/ah` | [Auction House](/guides/auction-house) for selling to anyone |

1. Stand within 10 blocks of the player.
2. Type `/trade <player>` (or Shift + Right-click them).
3. They have 60 seconds to accept.
4. Both of you add items, money or coins in the trade window.
5. Both confirm. There is a 5-second countdown before it completes.

## Staying safe

- Read the window before you confirm. Check the item names and amounts.
- If you move away, run a command or interact with something, the trade is cancelled.
- Only trade with people you trust, and never agree to "send first".
- Not sure what something is worth? Look it up in the [Auction House](/guides/auction-house) first.

## Which one should I use?

- `/trade`: instant, with someone beside you
- Auction House (`/ah`): sell to anyone, even when you're offline
- [Player Shop](/guides/player-shops): a chest that sells for you 24/7
