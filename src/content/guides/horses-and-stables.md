Buy a horse and keep it in your stable. The stable has **5 slots**. Horses are sold by the Horse Stables NPCs. Higher tiers are better horses.

## Horse prices

| Tier | Prices (each horse) |
| --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | $1,000 / $1,500 / $2,000 / $2,500 / $3,000 |
| Tier 2 | $8,000 / $10,000 / $12,000 / $14,000 / $16,000 |
| Tier 3 | $60,000 / $70,000 / $80,000 / $90,000 / $100,000 |

## Horse armor

Dress up your horse in the CoinShop:

| Armor | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Leather | 500 coins |
| Iron | 1,000 coins |
| Golden | 2,500 coins |
| Diamond | 5,000 coins |

Open the horse armor menu to equip or remove it. Horse armor is cosmetic.

> Tip: save for a Tier 2 horse before you worry about armor. [Jobs](/guides/jobs) and the [Courier](/guides/courier) are good ways to build up money.
