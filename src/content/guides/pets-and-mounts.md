Pets follow you around. Mounts can be ridden.

**Where to get them:** the [CoinShop](/guides/coinshop). Prices are in coins.

- Veggie pets (carrot, potato, onion, broccoli, eggplant, tomato): 350 coins each
- Dinosaur pets: 800 coins each
- Dragon mount: 3,500 coins

## Using them

Once you buy one, you own it for good. Open `/pets` to summon yours. Pets are cosmetic. They never change how strong you are.

For horses, see [Horses and Stables](/guides/horses-and-stables).
