BrindleWood has about **80 custom enchantments** on top of vanilla, powered by ExcellentEnchants.

## Where to find them

- Enchanting tables
- Loot found in the world
- Gear carried by mobs

They show up on books and gear like any enchant.

## Examples

- **Veinminer** (up to level 3) mines whole ore veins
- **Telekinesis** sends drops straight to your inventory
- **Treefeller** fells whole trees
- **Replanter** replants crops
- **Smelter** smelts drops as you mine
- **Tunnel** and **Blast Mining** dig wider areas
- **Vampire** heals you when you hit

## Curses

Some enchants are curses, like Curse of Breaking. Check an item before you use it.
