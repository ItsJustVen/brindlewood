Need money now? Take out a loan. You get the cash right away and pay it back over time.

| Loan | Amount | Payment every 4 hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| #1 | $1,000 | $150 |
| #2 | $5,000 | $500 |
| #3 | $10,000 | $750 |
| #4 | $25,000 | $1,500 |
| #5 | $50,000 | $2,500 |
| #6 | $100,000 | $5,000 |

## How it works

- Interest of **2.5%** is added to the loan every 4 hours.
- Payments come out of your bank or your player account until the loan is paid back.
- If you can't afford a payment, the Banker warns you. Keep money available.
- Bigger loans are locked behind your **Trustworthy** trait level, so everyone's limit is different.

## Tips

- Only borrow what you can pay back.
- Higher loans can cost a lot in interest over time.
- Build up your Trustworthy trait to unlock bigger loans. See [Your Player Profile and Traits](/guides/player-profile-and-traits).
