A backpack is extra storage that follows you around.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/backpack` | Opens your backpack |
| `/enderchest` | Opens your ender chest from anywhere (also `/echest` or `/ec`) |

`/enderchest` is an unlockable command: reach character level 39, become King, or buy it in the [CoinShop](/guides/coinshop). Your ender chest is separate from your backpack, so use both for extra room.

Order tiers from the **Tailor NPC** in the spawn world at **82, 91, 120**. Each tier is owned permanently once it's made.

## Tiers

| Tier | Slots | Price | Base crafting time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 9 | $5,000 | 1 hour |
| 2 | 18 | $20,000 | 2 hours |
| 3 | 27 | $50,000 | 8 hours |
| 4 | 36 | $100,000 | 14 hours |
| 5 | 45 | $250,000 | 24 hours |
| 6 | 54 | $500,000 | 40 hours |

## Crafting time and price

Backpacks take real time to make, then you claim them from the Tailor. The Tailor's **Workload** (1 to 5) adds more time and raises the price, so a free Tailor is cheaper and faster.

## Tips

- Higher tiers need the **Pack Mule** trait. The Tailor menu shows what you need. See [Your Player Profile and Traits](/guides/player-profile-and-traits).
- The **Negotiator** trait lowers prices.
- Start the long builds before you log off.
