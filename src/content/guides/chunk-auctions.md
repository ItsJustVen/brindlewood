Need more land? Bid on it. The Landlord NPC auctions off chunks, and the winner gets them added to their claim limit.

## How it works

1. Talk to the Landlord in Aldoria, in the spawn world at **-28, 98, 38**.
2. Browse the open lots. Each lot is 2 to 20 chunks.
3. Place a bid with your money ($).
4. When the timer ends, the highest bidder wins and the chunks are added to your total.

## Prices and timing

- Bids start at about **$250 per chunk**.
- Lots come in small, medium and large sizes (2 to 5, 6 to 10 and 11 to 20 chunks).
- New lots appear about every 10 minutes, so check back often.
- You get a message if an auction you bid on is won by someone else.

## Tips

- Save up from [Jobs](/guides/jobs) and the [Courier](/guides/courier) before you bid.
- Extra chunks are also sold in the store, but you never have to pay for them.
