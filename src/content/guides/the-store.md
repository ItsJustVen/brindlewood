The store is optional. BrindleWood is built as support without buying the win. Almost everything sold can also be earned in-game, including claims and chunks.

[Open the store](https://brindlewood.tebex.io)

## What you'll find

- Coins (see [Coins](/guides/coins))
- Ranks (see [Ranks](/guides/ranks))
- Crate keys
- Fly Command (fly anywhere)
- Extra claims and chunks
- Premium races
- Furniture package
- Spawner Core

## Paid only

Three things can't be earned in-game:

- Premium races
- Fly Command (flying anywhere on the server). Flying inside your own claims can be earned in-game.
- Spawner Core (spawner permissions, premium only since too many spawners cause lag)

Everything else, including ranks, claims, crates and cosmetics, can be earned in-game if you'd rather play for it.

## Having trouble with a purchase?

Open a ticket in our Discord and tell us your username and what you bought.

**Bedrock players:** enter your name with the `BR_` prefix at checkout (for example `BR_Steve`), or your purchase won't reach the right account.
