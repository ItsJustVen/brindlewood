BrindleWood uses SimpleClaimSystem. Claim your land in playworld, and it stays yours.

New players start with:

- **1 claim**
- **10 chunks** in total
- Up to **10 trusted members** per claim
- Claiming is free

> Claim before you build. Unclaimed land can't be protected or restored.

## Commands

- `/claim` to claim the chunk you're standing in and open the claim menu
- `/unclaim` to give a claim up
- `/claims` to see your claims

`/territory`, `/unterritory` and `/territories` work the same way.

From the claim menu you can manage members and settings. Only add people you truly trust, and read [Griefing and Rollbacks](/guides/griefing-and-rollbacks) first.

## Rules

- You can only claim in **playworld**. Aldoria, the Nether and the End can't be claimed.
- **Use it or lose it:** claims are purged after 30 days without logging in.
- More claims come from ranks. Knight gives +2, Lord +4, Paladin +6, Duke +8 and King +10.
- More chunks come from [Chunk Auctions](/guides/chunk-auctions).
