Five premium ranks, each with a prefix in chat and TAB, a kit, extra slots and commands. Commands carry up the ranks. The slot numbers below are the total for that rank.

## Extra slots

| Rank | Homes | Claims | Auction | Player warps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Knight | +4 | +2 | +2 | +1 |
| Lord | +6 | +4 | +3 | +2 |
| Paladin | +8 | +6 | +4 | +2 |
| Duke | +10 | +8 | +5 | +3 |
| King | +12 | +10 | +6 | +3 |

## Where to buy a rank

The rank NPCs are in the spawn world:

- Knight: **0, 99, 53**
- Lord: **-3, 99, 52**
- Paladin: **-6, 99, 49**
- Duke: **-9, 99, 46**
- King: **-13, 99, 45**

## Rank commands

- **Knight:** `/disposal`, `/nick`
- **Lord:** adds `/back`
- **Paladin:** adds `/craft`
- **Duke:** adds `/feed`
- **King:** adds `/enderchest`

## Rank kits

Every rank has its own kit with about 14 to 16 items. Claim it in `/kits`. Each kit has a **30-day cooldown**.

## How to get a rank

**Coins (CoinShop)**

| Rank | Permanent | 30-day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Knight | 4,000 | 2,000 |
| Lord | 6,000 | 3,000 |
| Paladin | 8,000 | 4,000 |
| Duke | 10,000 | 5,000 |
| King | 12,000 | 6,000 |

Upgrade: 2,000 coins per step, one at a time. You can also buy ranks in the [store](https://brindlewood.tebex.io).

**Crates:** Common, Rare, Epic and Superior crates have a small chance to drop a 30-day Lord, Paladin, Duke or King rank.
