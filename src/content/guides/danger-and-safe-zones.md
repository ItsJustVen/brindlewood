Aldoria, the spawn world, has both. The area around Moonfall is a safe zone. Beyond it are danger zones. Signs and borders tell you when you cross from one to the other.

- **Safe Zone:** PvP is disabled. You can't leave while you are in combat.
- **Danger Zone:** mobs can spawn and hurt you, and other players can attack you. It works like the playworld, except you can't build or break blocks.

## Good to know

- Moonfall and the area around it are safe. Beyond the signs, treat Aldoria like the playworld and bring gear.
- Never log out in combat. See [PvP and Combat](/guides/pvp-and-combat).
- Claims can have their own PvP setting. See [Claiming Land](/guides/claiming-land).
