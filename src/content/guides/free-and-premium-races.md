## The four free races

These four are free for everyone, and each has an Awakened form at level 100.

### Ignari

Born of ash and ember.

- Fully immune to fire and lava
- At home in the forges and the Nether's heat
- Slowed and hungry while in water

### Zephyrin

Light as the wind.

- Fall damage greatly reduced
- Faster movement
- Feel heavy and slow deep underground, with mining fatigue below Y 40

### Marisel

Children of the tide.

- Water breathing and a swimmer's grace
- Their skin dries out far from water
- Slowed and weakened in deserts and badlands

### Terrune

Hewn from stone.

- Extra health, knockback resistance and mining speed
- Slow on foot
- They sink in deep water

## The four premium races

Available through the [store](/guides/the-store). Each has an Awakened form at level 100.

### Kaldrun

Forged in deep forges. Living armor.

- Heavy armor and heavy knockback resistance
- They sink in water and are slow on land

### Vyressa

Brewed in venom.

- Immune to poison and wither, and bonus attack damage
- Fragile health, and they can't drink milk

### Aethran

Born under far stars. They drift rather than fall.

- Virtually no fall damage, plus bonus luck and XP
- Slowed deep underground, and they can't sleep

### Umbrath

Born of the Void. They slip between shadows.

- Faster and stronger at night
- Fragile armor, and weakened in direct sunlight
