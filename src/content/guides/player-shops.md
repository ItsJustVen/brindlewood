Set up a chest that sells an item for you 24/7. Players walk up, click, and buy. A floating hologram shows what you're selling and the price.

There are **no commands** for player shops. Everything is done with the chest.

## Setting one up

1. Craft a **PlayerShop chest**: put a chest in the center of the crafting table and surround it with copper nuggets.
2. Place it in the world. Shops only work in **playworld** and the **shop world**, not the Nether, End, or Spawn.
3. **Shift + Right-click** the shop to set it up.
4. Enter your price when asked.
5. Put the items you want to sell inside the shop chest.

## Buying from a shop

- Right-click the shop.
- Click the item you want and confirm.

## Rules

- Every player can have **5 shops** (default limit).
- Shops can't be merged together (no double chests).
- A price of $0 is not allowed.
- If a shop's chest is empty, it shows as not ready.
- The money goes straight to you when someone buys.

## Tips

- Put shops near busy areas so more people see them.
- Place shops inside your [claim](/guides/claiming-land) so only you can touch the chest.
- Link a [player warp](/guides/warps) so customers can find you.
- Check the [Auction House](/guides/auction-house) for a price before setting yours.
- Stock one item per shop so the hologram is easy to read.
