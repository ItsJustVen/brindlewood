The Moonfall Town Bank is in Aldoria. The banker, Oswin Grudgeteller, counts every coin twice. No one likes him, but they all use his vault.

## Where to find them

Bankers are in the spawn world (Aldoria):

- Banker 1: **68, 92, 83**
- Banker 2: **-95, 80, 419**

## What you can do

- Deposit money (always tax-free)
- Withdraw money (a **2.5% tax** applies)
- **Banknotes:** turn money into a paper note you can trade. Minimum $100
- **Loans:** see [Bank Loans](/guides/bank-loans)
- **Guild bank:** see [Guilds](/guides/guilds)

You can open your personal account at any bank.

## Interest

- Your personal account pays **2.0% base interest every 8 hours**.
- The Trustworthy trait on your profile raises it.
- You only get interest while you're online.
- The guild account pays a fixed **2.5% every 8 hours**.

## Tips

- Don't carry more money than you need. Deposit it.
- Deposit before a dungeon run or a fight.
- Check your bank menu to see your balance, debt and current rate.
