Server Rules

Welcome to BrindleWood! 🌿 Our goal is to create a friendly, welcoming community where everyone can relax, build, explore, and have fun. By participating in our Discord or Minecraft servers, you agree to follow these rules.

  • 🌿 Be Respectful
    Treat everyone with kindness and respect. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, hate speech, threats, and targeted behavior are not allowed. Respect boundaries and keep disagreements civil.
  • 💬 Keep Chat Comfortable
    Keep conversations appropriate and use channels correctly. No spam, excessive profanity, NSFW/graphic content, or intentional disruption.

    🌎 English is required in public BrindleWood communication, including server chat and public Discord channels. This allows staff to properly understand conversations, assist players, and perform moderation duties. Other languages may be used in private conversations.

  • 🌎 No Real-World Debates
    BrindleWood is a place to enjoy Minecraft. Avoid arguments or debates about politics, religion, LGBTQ+ issues, or other divisive real-world topics. Sharing personal experiences is fine; turning them into debates is not.
  • ⛏️ Play Fair
    No cheating, hacked clients, exploits, unauthorized automation, or scams. Follow each server's gameplay rules.
  • 🏡 Respect the World
    No griefing, stealing, or damaging another player's builds without permission. Respect claims, towns, kingdoms, and community projects.
  • 📢 No Unauthorized Advertising
    Do not advertise other servers, communities, services, or websites without staff approval.
  • 🛡️ Respect Staff
    Follow reasonable staff instructions. If you disagree with a decision, contact staff privately rather than arguing publicly or harassing staff.
  • 🔒 Privacy & Safety
    Do not share personal information, threaten others, scam members, or attempt to access another person's account or private information.
  • 🧠 Common Sense
    Not every situation can be listed. Staff may act against behavior that harms the community, disrupts others, or attempts to bypass these rules.

⚖️ Enforcement

Breaking the rules may result in warnings, mutes, kicks, or temporary/permanent bans depending on the severity and circumstances.

❤️ Thank you for helping make BrindleWood a place everyone can enjoy!
