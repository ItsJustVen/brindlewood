Put a price on another player's head, or claim a bounty someone else placed.

## Placing a bounty

1. Type `/bounty <player> <amount>` (example: `/bounty Steve 5000`).
2. Confirm when asked.

The player doesn't need to be online. A **10% tax** is added to the amount, so budget for it.

## Claiming a bounty

- Kill the player who has the bounty on them.
- The bounty money is paid to you right away.
- Bounties don't expire. They stay until someone claims them.

## Tips

- PvP is on outside spawn. See [PvP and Combat](/guides/pvp-and-combat) for the combat timer and safe areas.
- Claimed land may have PvP turned off by its owner.
