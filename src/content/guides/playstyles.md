Your playstyle shapes your stats. It's separate from your race and your class, so you get all three.

Open the menu with `/playstyles`, or visit the Playstyles NPC in the spawn world at **8, 99, 18**. Choosing a playstyle gives you a **100-point stat boost** spread across your 10 stats. The menu shows stat bars, so you can preview each one before you pick. **Switching is free**, so try one out and change it if it doesn't fit.

## The eight playstyles

| Playstyle | Style |
| --- | --- |
| Warrior | A fearless fighter who excels at close combat |
| Wizard | A master of magic, using enchantments, potions and mystical tools |
| Cleric | A compassionate healer who supports and protects others |
| Survivor | Endurance, gathering resources, building bases and surviving threats |
| Adventurer | An eager explorer who loves discovering land and hidden treasure |
| Scholar | Deep knowledge of crafting techniques and game mechanics |
| Opportunist | Clever and resourceful |
| Architect | Focused on building |

Not sure? Pick the one that matches what you're going to spend most of your time doing.
