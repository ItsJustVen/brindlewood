When you die, your items are stored in a **grave** where you died, instead of scattering on the ground. Go back and interact with it to get your items.

- A glow marks your grave and is visible from about 30 blocks away.
- Other players can't break your grave.
- You can have up to **10 graves** at once. When you die a time too many, your oldest grave breaks.
- You do not keep all your experience when you die.
- Custom cosmetic armor and tool sets stay in your inventory when you die.

## Staying alive

- Difficulty is Hard, so bring food and spare armor on trips.
- `/back` takes you to where you died, once you unlock it at level 17 or buy it in the CoinShop.
- Use `/sethome` before you explore so you can always get back.
- Never log out in combat. You die and drop your items. See [PvP and Combat](/guides/pvp-and-combat).
- Deposit money at the bank before dungeon runs and fights. See [Banks and the Banker](/guides/banks).
