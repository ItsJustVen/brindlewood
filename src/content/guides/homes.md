Set a spot you can teleport back to any time.

- `/sethome <name>` sets a home where you stand
- `/home <name>` teleports you there
- `/delhome <name>` removes one
- `/homes` lists your homes

Set a home before you go exploring so you can always get back.

## How many homes?

Every player starts with **2 homes**. You get more from:

- **Character levels:** +1 home at 26 different levels
- **Ranks:** Knight +4, Lord +6, Paladin +8, Duke +10, King +12

## Good to know

- Teleports have a 3-second delay. Stand still until it finishes.
- You can't teleport while tagged in combat. See [PvP and Combat](/guides/pvp-and-combat).
