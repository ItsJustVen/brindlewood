Spawners are a big part of BrindleWood, and they run on SpawnerMeta. Every player can mine spawners with **Silk Touch**. There's no rank or purchase required.

## What you can do with them

- Stack spawners together
- Change what a spawner spawns
- Customize the spawn rate
- Use the spawner shop
- And a lot more

## Spawner commands

- `/spawnerview` see all your spawners and their stats
- `/spawnershop` buy spawners
- `/spawnerdrops` get back spawner drops if your inventory was full
- `/spawnerlocations` view all your placed spawners
- `/spawnertrust` manage the trusted-player list for your spawners

## Spawner Core

Spawner Core is a store item that gives extra spawner permissions. Too many spawners cause lag, so the extra tier is paid. It's one of the few things that can't be earned in-game. See [The Store](/guides/the-store).
