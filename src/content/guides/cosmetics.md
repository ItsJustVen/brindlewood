Cosmetics change how you look. They never change how strong you are.

All **100 coins each** in the [CoinShop](/guides/coinshop):

- Hats (18)
- Particles (15)
- Death effects (3)
- Projectile effects (17)
- Chat tags (about 250)
- Tool skins

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/cosmetics` | Opens the cosmetics menu (hats, particles, death and projectile effects) |
| `/tags` | Opens your chat tags |
| `/toolskins` | Opens your tool skins |
| `/hiddenarmor toggle` | Hides or shows your armor on your character |
| `/hiddenarmor hide` / `show` | Hides or shows it directly |

## What they do

- **Hats:** wear one on your head
- **Particles:** a trail of effects as you move
- **Death effects:** how you go out, with style
- **Projectile effects:** arrows and more leave a trail
- **Chat tags:** a title beside your name
- **Tool skins:** see [Tool Skins](/guides/tool-skins)

Once you buy a cosmetic, it's yours for good. Coins are free to earn, so you never have to spend real money.
