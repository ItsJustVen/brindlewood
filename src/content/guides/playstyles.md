Your playstyle shapes your stats and gives you three traits. It's separate from your race and your class, so you get all three.

Open the menu with `/playstyles`, or visit the Playstyles NPC in the spawn world at **8, 99, 18**. The menu shows stat bars, so you can preview each playstyle before you pick.

> **You can change your playstyle, but there is a one-week cooldown.** Read each one first.

![The Playstyles menu showing the Warrior](/screens/guide-playstyles.webp)

## How the stat boost works

You have 10 stats: Speed, Strength, Health, Endurance, Resilience, Mining Speed, Attack Speed, Reach, Luck and Wisdom. Each starts at 20. Choosing a playstyle gives you a **100-point boost** shared out across them, and every playstyle shares it out differently.

| Playstyle | Speed | Strength | Health | Endurance | Resilience | Mining | Attack | Reach | Luck | Wisdom |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Warrior** | +15 | +20 | +10 | +5 | +15 | – | +25 | +5 | +5 | – |
| **Wizard** | +5 | – | +15 | +10 | +15 | +5 | – | +15 | +15 | +20 |
| **Cleric** | +10 | +10 | +25 | +10 | +10 | +10 | +10 | – | +10 | +5 |
| **Survivor** | +5 | +20 | +15 | +15 | +20 | +10 | – | +10 | – | +5 |
| **Adventurer** | +20 | +5 | – | +25 | +10 | +10 | +10 | +10 | +5 | +5 |
| **Scholar** | +5 | +5 | +5 | +15 | +10 | +5 | +5 | +10 | +15 | +25 |
| **Opportunist** | +10 | +15 | +5 | – | – | +20 | +15 | +10 | +20 | +5 |
| **Architect** | +10 | – | +10 | +10 | – | +20 | +5 | +25 | +5 | +15 |

A dash means no boost for that stat.

## Traits

Each playstyle also gives you three traits. Traits get stronger as your tier goes up, from Bronze to Master. See [Your Player Profile and Traits](/guides/player-profile-and-traits).

| Playstyle | Style | Traits you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Warrior** | A fearless close-combat fighter | Gladiator: more damage against players. Leader: more members allowed in your guild. Iron Fist: more damage barehanded. |
| **Wizard** | Magic, enchanting and potions | Wizard Enchanting: a chance to spend 1 less XP point when enchanting. Enderman Attack: hit a player with an ender pearl to damage them. Vampire Healing: heal when you kill players. |
| **Cleric** | A healer who supports others | Divine Recovery: Regeneration II when your health gets low. Healing Touch: right-click a player to give them Regeneration I. Featherweight: bonus damage (open `/traits` to see when). |
| **Survivor** | Endurance, resources and staying alive | Olympic Swimmer: faster swimming. Berserker: more damage when you're below 2 hearts. Netherborn: a boost to all 10 stats while in the Nether. |
| **Adventurer** | An explorer who loves new land and treasure | Wayfarer: more homes with `/sethome`. Pathfinder: more deliveries shown at the couriers. Equestrian: faster horse riding. |
| **Scholar** | Deep knowledge of crafting and mechanics | Archeologist: a better chance of finding historical artifacts. Cartographer: a shorter random teleport cooldown. Endborn: a boost to all 10 stats while in the End. |
| **Opportunist** | Clever, resourceful and good at trading | Negotiator: cheaper prices from NPCs. Diamond Seeker: more diamonds from ore, like Fortune. Scavenger: a chance to find gold nuggets when digging dirt. |
| **Architect** | A builder | Artisan: lets you use the iron chisel for detailed carving. Ore Prospector: more yield from ores, like Fortune. Toolsmith: tools lose less durability. |

Pick the one that matches how you expect to spend most of your time.
