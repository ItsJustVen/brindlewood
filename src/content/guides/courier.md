Couriers at spawn hand out delivery requests. Bring them the items they ask for and get paid in money ($). Talk to a Courier NPC (like Talon Briskfell) to see the requests that are open.

## How it works

1. Open the Courier menu and pick a request.
2. Gather the items it asks for.
3. Hand them in before the request expires.
4. The items are taken from your inventory and your reward is paid.

## Delivery types

Every request is one of three types, and the type decides the bonus you get on top of the base pay:

| Type | Bonus | Stays open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard (most common) | Normal pay, plus a small random bonus | About 45 to 75 minutes |
| Express | 2x the bonus | About 8 to 12 minutes |
| Lightning (rare) | 4x the bonus | About 60 to 120 minutes |

If you see a Lightning request, grab it. New requests appear about every 10 minutes.

## Courier demand

Couriers show a demand level from 1 to 5. It changes without warning. When demand is high there are more requests to take, so it's the best time to earn.

## Tips

- Check what a request wants before you commit, so you don't chase a rare item you can't get in time.
- Keep common items on hand (ingots, logs, crops) so Express requests are easy money.
- Express requests close fast, so they're best for items you already own.
- Couriers are a good use of drops from jobs and dungeons.
