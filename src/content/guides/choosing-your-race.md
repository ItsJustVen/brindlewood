Your race defines how your character plays. Each one plays differently, with its own strengths, weaknesses and abilities.

![The Choose Your Destiny menu, where you pick your race](/screens/guide-race-menu.webp)

## Picking your race

- The race menu opens on its own the first time you join.
- You can't play until you pick one.
- **Your race is permanent.** You can't switch between the free races, so choose with care.
- The only way to get a different race is the store: each premium race is **$10** and is bought separately, and you can't switch between premium races either. A **custom race** (you design it, staff approve it) starts at **$35**.
- Missed it or want another look? Use `/races gui`.

## Race commands

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/races gui` | Opens the list of races to choose from |
| `/races info <race>` | Shows info about a race |
| `/races tree` | Shows the race evolution tree |
| `/races tree <race>` | Shows the tree for one race, like `/races tree Ignari` |
| `/races profile` | Shows your race, level and progress |
| `/races leaderboard` | Shows the race leaderboard |
| `/races help` | Lists the race commands |

![The race tree menu](/screens/guide-race-tree.webp)

## Growing your race

Your race levels up as you play, up to level 100. You get a new ability at level 50. At level 100 you unlock your race's **Awakened form**, with a stronger passive, special drops and an ability. See [Awakened Races](/guides/awakened-races).

## The free races at a glance

| Race | Strengths | Weakness | Level 50 | Level 100 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ignari** | Immune to fire and lava. Bonus attack damage that grows with your level. Bonus Nether drops: blaze rods, magma cream, glowstone, nether wart | Slowed and hungry in water | A brief burst of fire resistance | A fire nova burns nearby enemies |
| **Zephyrin** | Huge fall damage reduction and faster movement. Bonus drops: honeycomb, honey, phantom membranes, feathers | Mining fatigue below Y 40 | A temporary glide and slow-fall | A feather burst launches you forward |
| **Marisel** | Water breathing and Dolphin's Grace while swimming. Bonus health. Bonus drops: kelp, sea pickles, prismarine, guardian loot | Slowed and weakened in deserts and badlands | Longer Dolphin's Grace | Heals everyone nearby |
| **Terrune** | Bonus health, knockback resistance and faster block breaking. Bonus ore drops: iron, copper, gold, coal, ancient debris | Slowed while swimming | A temporary damage resistance | A ground slam knocks back and damages nearby enemies |

## Which race should I pick?

- Love the Nether and fire? **Ignari**
- Prefer the sky, speed and falling from high places? **Zephyrin**
- Water, the ocean and swimming? **Marisel**
- Mining, caves and a tough character? **Terrune**

The four free races and four premium races are in [Free and Premium Races](/guides/free-and-premium-races).
