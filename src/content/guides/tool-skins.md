Custom looks for your tools and weapons. They never change stats.

## Ways to get them

- **CoinShop:** 100 coins per skin
- **Character levels:** free tool skins at levels 4, 10, 18, 23, 30, 36, 42, 47, 52 and 57
- **Crates:** a random tool skin can drop from the Common, Rare, Epic, Superior and Vote crates

## What can be skinned

Swords, bows, fishing rods, shovels, pickaxes, hoes, crossbows and axes, with a page of skins for each.

## Using a skin voucher

1. Hold the voucher and click in the air. It tells you which skin you claimed.
2. Open `/toolskins`.
3. Apply the skin to the tool or weapon it's for.

> Careful: once a skin is on a tool, you can't change it. If that tool or weapon breaks, you lose the skin.
