The Four Kingdoms

One kingdom in each corner of the world. Choose where your story begins.

Northwest

Peacefield

Peace is the heart of Peacefield. This is a kingdom for players who value calm, cooperation, and a quiet corner of the world to call home, and its people will do everything in their power to protect that peace.

Northeast

Rosewood

The story of Rosewood is still being written. Check back soon, or ask about it in our Discord.

Southwest

Solyra

Ruled by the Goddess of Death herself, Solyra is a kingdom of Greek, Italian, and desert-inspired architecture. Its people are proud to work as one community, with shared storage, shared resources, and shared villagers. In Solyra, everyone builds together and everyone benefits.

Southeast

Subterra

What lies beneath the world belongs to those willing to dig for it. Subterra is a civilization below the surface: a growing community, a trading center, and a defensive stronghold, all carved from the depths.
