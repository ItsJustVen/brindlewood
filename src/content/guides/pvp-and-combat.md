## PvP

- Spawn (Aldoria) is safe. PvP is off in the spawn hub.
- Everywhere else, PvP is on (including playworld). Other players can fight you. Inside a claim, PvP depends on whether the claim owner turned it on.

Difficulty is Hard, so be ready for mobs too.

## Combat tagging

When you hit a player or get hit by one, you're in combat for **10 seconds**. A timer shows how long is left.

While tagged:

- **Don't log out.** If you disconnect during combat, you die and drop your items.
- No teleporting, flying or commands until the timer ends.

Dying or killing your opponent ends combat right away.

## Tips

- Move your valuables to your home chest before you go looking for a fight.
- Fighting for a bounty? See [Bounties](/guides/bounties).
