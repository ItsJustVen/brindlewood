Every vote helps new players find BrindleWood, and it pays you back. Each vote gives you:

- **1 Vote Crate key**
- **10 Level XP**

Type `/vote` in-game to see the vote links any time.

## Vote on all three sites

Every link counts:

- [minecraft-server-list.com](https://minecraft-server-list.com/server/503659/vote/)
- [minecraftservers.org](https://minecraftservers.org/vote/662534)
- [servertilt.com](https://www.servertilt.com/minecraft-servers/brindlewood-rpg/vote)

## How it works

1. Open a link above.
2. Enter your exact Minecraft username.
3. Complete the vote.
4. Your reward arrives in-game. If you're offline, you get it when you log in.

Each site has its own cooldown (usually once a day), so come back and vote again.

## Using your key

Use your Vote Crate key on the Vote Crate. See [Crates and Keys](/guides/crates-and-keys) for what crates can hold.
