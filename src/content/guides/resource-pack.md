BrindleWood requires its resource pack. It powers the custom furniture, cosmetics and every menu on the server, so the server can't be played without it.

It installs automatically. Accept the prompt when you join and wait for it to finish.

## How long does it take?

The first join downloads the pack, which takes about **45 seconds to 10 minutes** depending on your device and connection. Later joins are fast because the pack is saved.

Bedrock has a separate pack that also installs by itself.

## If the download seems stuck

- Don't cancel it, even if it looks frozen. Slower devices and connections can take several minutes.
- Use a stable connection for your first join.
- If you cancelled or declined, just rejoin and accept it this time.
- After a big server update the pack may re-download once.

> Still stuck? Open a ticket in our Discord with your device (PC, phone or console), Java or Bedrock, and what you see on screen.
