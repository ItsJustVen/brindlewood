BrindleWood is a small, volunteer-run Minecraft community. We collect as little as we can, only to run the server safely and keep it fair. This page covers our website, our Minecraft server, our Discord server and our Discord bot.

## This website

- **No accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies.** The website is a static site. We do not run analytics on it.
- **Server status.** The home page shows whether the server is online. To do that, your browser asks a public status service (mcstatus.io) about our server. That service can see your IP address, as any website you visit can.
- **Hosting.** The site is hosted by GitHub Pages and the domain is managed by Cloudflare. They may keep ordinary technical logs, under their own privacy policies.
- **Links to other services.** Our Discord, our Tebex store and our live map are run by other services with their own privacy policies.

## The Minecraft server

When you play, the server and its plugins record:

- Your **Minecraft username and UUID** (and your Xbox gamertag if you play on Bedrock).
- Your **IP address**, used for security, to enforce bans and to stop ban evasion.
- **Gameplay data**: things like your playtime, ranks, money and coins, claims, homes, guild membership, quests, skills and levels, and your builds.
- **Activity logs** of block changes, container use and commands. Staff use these to investigate griefing, theft and cheating, and to roll back damage.
- **Chat messages**, which are visible to other players and may be logged and shown on Discord.
- **Moderation records** such as warnings, mutes and bans, with the reason.

## Discord and linking your account

- If you join our Discord, Discord shows us your username, ID and the messages you post there.
- If you link your Minecraft account with the `/link` command, our bot stores the connection between your **Discord ID and your Minecraft account**, so it can give you roles and rewards.
- Support tickets are visible to the staff who handle them.

## Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by **Tebex**, not by BrindleWood. We never see or store your card or payment details. Tebex gives us the details of what you bought, your Minecraft username and the email on the order, so we can deliver your purchase and help with problems.

## How long we keep things

We keep gameplay and linking data while you are active. If you stop playing, we aim to remove non-safety data about you after about **12 months**. We keep **moderation records** and **purchase records** longer, because we need them to keep the community safe, handle disputes and meet our obligations.

## What we never do

- We do not sell your information.
- We do not share it with advertisers.
- We only share it when required by law, or to protect players from serious harm.

## Leaderboards and public pages

Player names on leaderboards and directories on this website are only shown for players who have chosen to appear. You can ask to be removed at any time.

## Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it (where we are not required to keep it). To ask, open a ticket in our Discord or message a staff member. We will answer as soon as a volunteer can.

## Children

BrindleWood is a Minecraft community, and young players join it. If you are under 13, please get a parent or guardian's permission before playing or joining our Discord. Parents can ask us to review or remove their child's information at any time through a Discord ticket.

## Changes

If we change this page, we will update the date at the top. Material changes will be announced in our Discord.
