Signs and borders tell you when PvP changes.

- **Safe Zone:** PvP is disabled beyond this point. You can't leave if you are in combat.
- **Danger Zone:** PvP is enabled beyond this point. Be careful, you may be killed.

## Good to know

- Spawn (Aldoria) is safe. Almost everywhere else is not.
- Never log out in combat. See [PvP and Combat](/guides/pvp-and-combat).
- Claims can have their own PvP setting. See [Claiming Land](/guides/claiming-land).
