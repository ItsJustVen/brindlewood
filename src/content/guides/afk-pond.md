Stand in the pond and get paid for doing nothing.

**Where:** Aldoria (the spawn world), outside the gate and to the right. **Coordinates:** x -34, y 101, z 46.5.

## What you earn while you stand in it

- **$50** every 15 minutes
- **1 coin** every 2 hours

## How it works

- You get a chat message when you enter the pond: you'll now receive AFK rewards.
- You get another when you leave: you'll no longer receive them.
- You have to stay inside the pond. Step out and the rewards stop.
- Rewards only come while you're logged in.

## Good to know

Coins from the pond are slow. For faster coins, check [Free Rewards](/guides/free-rewards) and [Character Levels](/guides/character-levels). Parking at the pond while you do something else (homework, dinner, a show) is exactly what it's for.
