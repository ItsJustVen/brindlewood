A class adds bonuses on top of your race. Your race, class and playstyle all stack.

## Opening the class menu

Use `/races classgui`. This menu doesn't open on its own, so run it yourself to pick your class.

**Switching:** you can change class, but there's a **24-hour cooldown** between picks. Choose with care. Every class has levels up to 100, and you'll get a message in chat each time you level up.

## The six classes

| Class | Style | Bonuses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | Frontline muscle | +4 Health, +1.5 Attack Damage, +1 Armor |
| Mage | Trades steel for power. Fragile, but never unarmed | -2 Health, -1 Attack Damage, +1 Luck |
| Ranger | Strikes before you know they're there | +2% Movement Speed, +0.5 Attack Damage, -1 Armor |
| Rogue | Fast, thin-skinned and gone before you swing back | +3% Movement Speed, +0.3 Attack Speed, -2 Health, -1 Armor |
| Cleric | Keeps the party standing | +2 Health and faster natural regeneration |
| Guardian | Immovable | +6 Health, +2 Armor, +1 Armor Toughness, +10% Knockback Resistance, -2% Speed |

## Which class should I pick?

- Want to be tanky? Guardian or Warrior.
- Want to move fast and hit hard? Ranger or Rogue.
- Want to support your group? Cleric.
- Want to try something different? Mage.

Your race matters too. A Terrune (extra health) Guardian and an Umbrath (night hunter) Rogue play very differently. Mix and match!
