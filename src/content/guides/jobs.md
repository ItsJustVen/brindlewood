Jobs are the main way to earn money ($) by playing normally. Mine, farm, fish or fight, and get paid for it.

**Start here:** each job has its own NPC in Aldoria, the spawn world. You walk there to join, and some are more than 500 blocks from the spawn gate, so it's a bit of a journey. Money arrives as you do the job's actions.

The main **Jobs NPC** at **-53, 99, 19** in the spawn world shows how many job slots you have, how many you're using and your job level. Once you've chosen a job, it can teleport you to your employer (that job's NPC).

## Joining and leaving

Jobs are **not** joined with commands. Walk up to the job's own NPC in Aldoria and use its menu to join, leave or check your progress.

## How jobs work

- You start with **1 job slot**.
- Your 2nd slot unlocks at character level 19, and your 3rd at level 49.
- Every job levels up as you work it, up to **level 30**.
- Higher job level means higher pay: at level 30 you earn about 3.9x what you earned at level 1.
- There is no hourly earnings cap.

## What each job pays

Pay depends on what you do. These are the base amounts at job level 1. They grow about 10% per level (see above). Some jobs pay a flat amount for everything they cover, and others pay far more for rare things.

| Job | What pays | Pay per action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alchemist | Brewing potions | $0.40 to $5 (dragon's breath pays the most) |
| Builder | Placing blocks | $0.50 per block |
| Enchanter | Enchanting items | $1 to $30 (an elytra pays the most, netherite gear $20) |
| Engineer | Crafting and repairing | $0.50 to $10 crafting (end crystals, ender chests and lodestones pay the most), up to $5 repairing netherite tools |
| Explorer | Exploring new places | $0.10 to $3 |
| Farmer | Harvesting and planting crops | $0.10 to $0.40 |
| Fighter | Killing mobs and players | $0.50 to $10 (killing a player pays the most, then a warden at $8) |
| Fisher | Fishing | $1 to $3.50 (pufferfish pays the most) |
| Guard | Killing Sanctum of Skulls monsters | $1 per kill |
| Hunter | Hunting and taming animals | $0.80 to $8 hunting (polar bears pay the most), $2.50 to $4 taming |
| Lumberjack | Chopping logs and planting saplings | $0.10 to $0.40 |
| Miner | Mining ores and smelting | $0.30 to $3.50 mining (diamond pays the most), $0.40 to $0.70 smelting |

## Tips

- Pick the job that matches what you already enjoy.
- Some jobs pay more per action (like Enchanter and Fighter); others pay less per action but are easy to do all day (like Farmer and Lumberjack).
- Spend your money at the [Auction House](/guides/auction-house), [Player Shops](/guides/player-shops), the Landlord's [chunk auctions](/guides/chunk-auctions) and the [horse stables](/guides/horses-and-stables).
- Don't forget the free hourly, daily and weekly rewards in `/rewards`.
