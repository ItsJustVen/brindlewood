Your race defines how your character plays. Each one plays differently, with its own strengths, weaknesses and abilities.

## Picking your race

- The race menu opens on its own the first time you join.
- You can't play until you pick one.
- Missed it or want another look? Use `/races gui`.

## Growing your race

Your race levels up as you play, up to level 100. At level 100 you unlock your race's **Awakened form**, with a stronger passive, special drops and an ability. See [Awakened Races](/guides/awakened-races).

## Which race should I pick?

- Love the Nether and fire? **Ignari**
- Prefer the sky, speed and falling from high places? **Zephyrin**
- Water, the ocean and swimming? **Marisel**
- Mining, caves and a tough character? **Terrune**

The four free races and four premium races are in [Free and Premium Races](/guides/free-and-premium-races).
