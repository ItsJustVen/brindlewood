## Sailors

Sailors like Finn Wavecrest at Moonfall Harbor will sail you to other destinations. Open the menu, pick a travel route, and set sail.

## Navigators

Navigators like Corin Seabound know the Nether. They teleport you to dangerous places there.

## Before you go

- Set a home first with `/sethome`.
- Bring food, armor and spare gear. Difficulty is Hard.
- PvP is on outside spawn, so valuables stay home.
- If you die, see [Dying: Graves and Item Loss](/guides/dying-graves-and-item-loss).
